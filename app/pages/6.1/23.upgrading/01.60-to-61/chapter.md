# UserFrosting 6.1

**UserFrosting 6.1** is the first minor release of the UserFrosting 6. While UserFrosting 6 brought major changes and improvements, especially for the UI, version 6.1 brings the first true new feature to UserFrosting. It also includes various enhancements and refinements to further refine the framework, including major improvements to Docker support.

This section walks you through the upgrade process step-by-step. Changes are less substantial than the initial 6.0 release, but still require careful attention.

Before proceeding, ensure you're running the **latest version of UserFrosting 6.0**.
